## Parties

1. **Virginia State University** ("VSU"), the host institution.
2. **The Venture** — Dr. Shawn M. Nicholson LLC ("Nicholson LLC") as
   **prime contractor**, contracting directly with VSU on behalf of the
   Venture, with Builder Tech LLC, VoltPop, and Explay as **subcontractors**
   under Nicholson LLC. Internal structure, roles, and governance among
   these four are set out in `Joint-Venture-Agreement-Draft.md`, not here.

VSU is **not** a Party to the Joint Venture Agreement or the Curriculum
MOU — this document is the only one VSU actually signs.

## 1. Purpose and Scope of Engagement

VSU engages the Venture, through Nicholson LLC as prime contractor, to
design, develop, and deliver the educational program currently referred
to in this KB by the placeholder name "VSU Innovation Program" (see
[Builder Tech's program-name item](Builder-Tech-Workspace.md#program-name) —
actual name not yet decided), including the Tri-Cities Career Showcase
culminating event.

Curriculum development, instructional delivery, and technical build
work are performed by Builder Tech, VoltPop, and Explay as subcontractors,
per their own Scope of Work Exhibits under the Curriculum Development
MOU — **not restated here.**
