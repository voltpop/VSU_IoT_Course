## VSU Innovation Program

**One-line blurb:**
A semester-long, project-based program at Virginia State University
where students build real AI- and IoT-powered business solutions to
real Tri-Cities-region problems.

**Short about (1-2 sentences):**
The VSU Innovation Program is a hands-on, project-based course where
Virginia State University students design and build a working
prototype — combining generative AI, IoT hardware, and entrepreneurship
— to solve a real business problem rooted in the Tri-Cities region.

**Long about (press/partner-facing):**
The VSU Innovation Program is a 14-week, project-based program offered
by Virginia State University in partnership with Builder Tech LLC,
Explay, and VoltPop LLC. Students root their projects in the Tri-Cities
regional economy, applying generative AI, IoT, and cybersecurity
concepts to a real business opportunity or problem — building a working
prototype, a pitch deck, a professional portfolio, and a resume along
the way. The program culminates in a public product launch, a live
pitch before judges, and a Career Day connecting students directly to
regional employers. It runs during the VSU Spring 2027 semester
(January 19 – April 29, 2027).
