# VSU Innovation Program — Stakeholder Notes

**This is research/contact prep, not a pitch deck.** For each
stakeholder: what they want or care about, how to reach them, current
outreach status, and (once known) a tailored framing angle. The actual
tailored presentation gets built later, from these notes, once enough
is known per stakeholder — not guessed at generically ahead of time.
Full funding-source reasoning and the underlying budget figures live in
`Admin-Business-Legal.md` §8/§6.

## Framing — read before any conversation

Stakeholder outreach is happening **before** VSU's own institutional
advancement/fundraising office has formally signed off. Foundations and
corporate funders typically expect the university itself to be the
applicant/recipient of record, and a public university's advancement
office usually wants to coordinate/approve external asks made in its
name.

- Frame every conversation right now as **exploratory/preliminary** —
  "we're building a program with VSU and exploring what partnership
  could look like" — not a firm ask made on VSU's behalf.
- Don't quote the total program cost (currently ~$295K–$311K, and still
  has open line items — see `Admin-Business-Legal.md` §6/§9) as a fixed,
  approved ask. Treat it as a working estimate.
- Be ready to say plainly that anything formal routes through VSU's own
  channels once its advancement office is looped in.
- Dr. Shawn Nicholson is VSU's actual Institutional Liaison — his
  presence/blessing on a call lends real legitimacy; worth considering
  for conversations beyond the earliest exploratory ones.

## Reusable program talking points (raw material for the eventual tailored deck)

Not stakeholder-specific — common facts likely to appear in any tailored
presentation once one gets built. Kept here so this research doesn't
have to be redone; **do not present as a finished deck.**

- **The opportunity:** VSU (an HBCU at the center of the Tri-Cities
  region) has students with deep domain knowledge of the region's
  economy but limited structured paths into applying AI/IoT to solve
  problems in exactly those industries.
- **What students build:** a working IoT-based/broader prototype (live
  mobile web app with real hardware integration), a pitch/slide deck,
  a resume/portfolio/elevator pitch, and direct exposure to employers
  via Career Day.
- **Why this team, why now:** Builder Tech (Program Director + BI/Design
  instruction), Explay (curriculum + Entrepreneurship/Innovation
  instruction), VoltPop (curriculum + Engineering/CS instruction),
  and VSU (host institution) — plus an existing MoU between VSU's
  College of Engineering and School of Business giving this
  institutional precedent, not a cold start.
- **Regional fit:** students root projects in Tri-Cities industries
  (advanced manufacturing, logistics, food & beverage, defense/
  government logistics, clean energy, controlled-environment
  agriculture, data centers) — matches the kind of regional impact
  partners like VGR, Cameron Foundation, and regional employers are
  built to support.
- **Timeline:** ~3-month Setup Period, then the VSU Spring 2027
  semester (Jan 19 – Apr 29, 2027), 14 instructional weeks, culminating
  in a public product launch, a live pitch before judges, and a
  Career Day.
- **Investment ask (still in flux):** total program cost currently
  ~$295K–$311K (open items remain — see `Admin-Business-Legal.md`
  §6/§9); VSU can't absorb this alone as an HBCU; in-kind opportunities
  already identified (VSU-provided laptops, campus space, existing
  software licenses, potential hardware-vendor kit donations, AI-tooling
  credits) reduce the cash ask. Per-stakeholder ask amount/type (cash,
  in-kind, hardware, mentors) still needs deciding per stakeholder above.
- **Why this matters:** portfolio-grade outcomes for students often
  first-generation/first-in-tech/underrepresented in these industries;
  a direct VSU-talent-to-employer pipeline; a repeatable model where a
  successful first cohort builds the case for future semesters and
  deeper regional investment.
