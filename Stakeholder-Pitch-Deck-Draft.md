## 1. Title

**The VSU Innovation Program**
A semester-long, hands-on innovation program at Virginia State University
combining AI, IoT hardware, and entrepreneurship — built for and rooted
in the Tri-Cities region.
*[Partner logos: VSU, Builder Tech, Explay, VoltPop — to be sourced]*

## 2. The Opportunity

- Virginia State University is a Historically Black College/University
  (HBCU) at the center of the Tri-Cities region (Petersburg, Hopewell,
  Colonial Heights, Prince George, Dinwiddie, Chesterfield).
- Students here have deep domain knowledge of the region's economy —
  advanced manufacturing, logistics, food & beverage, defense/government
  logistics, clean energy, data centers — but limited structured paths
  into applying emerging tech (AI, IoT) to solve problems in exactly
  those industries.
- The Innovation Program closes that gap: students build a real
  business solution to a real regional problem, using AI-assisted
  development and IoT hardware, over one semester.

## 3. What Students Actually Build

By the end of the program, each student/team has:
- A working IoT-based (or broader) prototype — a live, functional
  mobile web app, with real hardware integration.
- A pitch/slide deck covering target market, financial model, and
  business fundamentals.
- A completed resume, portfolio, and elevator pitch.
- Direct exposure to real employers via Career Day.

This isn't a lecture course. It's project-based: every week adds a
layer to one evolving, real product — from systems thinking and problem
discovery, through hardware and software build, to a public launch and
a live pitch in front of judges.

## 5. Regional Fit — Built For the Tri-Cities, Not Adapted To It

Students choose a business problem rooted in the Tri-Cities region and
its key industries (advanced manufacturing, logistics & distribution,
food & beverage processing, defense/government logistics, clean energy,
controlled-environment agriculture, data centers). The program is
designed so its economic development benefit stays local — matching
exactly the kind of regional impact partners like Virginia Gateway
Region, Cameron Foundation, and regional employers are built to support.

## 6. Program Timeline

- **Setup Period**: ~3 months of curriculum design and program setup
  before the semester (already underway).
- **Program Dates**: **January 19 – April 29, 2027** (VSU Spring 2027
  semester).
- 14 instructional weeks, Tuesday/Thursday core classes, plus office
  hours and professional development sessions.
- Culminates in a public product launch, a live pitch before judges,
  and a **Career Day** connecting students directly to regional
  employers.

## 7. Investment Ask

- Total program cost: **approximately $295,000–$311,000** (final figure
  in progress — labor is the dominant cost, ~79% of the budget, in line
  with a hands-on instructional program of this scope, not a standard
  single-course engagement).
- **VSU cannot absorb this cost alone** as an HBCU — this program is
  built to run on a mix of VSU support, in-kind partner contributions,
  and external funding.
- **In-kind opportunities already identified** reduce the cash ask
  meaningfully: VSU-provided laptops, campus space, and existing
  software licenses; potential hardware-vendor kit donations; and
  active outreach to Anthropic's education/nonprofit programs for
  AI-tooling credits.
- **What we're asking partners for**: [amount to be tailored per
  stakeholder — cash funding, in-kind sponsorship (hardware, event

## 8. Why This Matters

- Concrete, portfolio-grade outcomes for students who are often
  first-generation, first-in-tech, or otherwise underrepresented in the
  industries this program touches.
- Direct pipeline between VSU talent and regional employers, addressing
  the same workforce gap those employers already face.
- A repeatable model: a successful first cohort creates the case for
  future semesters, more employer partners, and deeper regional
  investment.
