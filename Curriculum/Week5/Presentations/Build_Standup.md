# Build Stand-Up

*Source: [`../Day2.md`](../Day2.md) item 1. ~15 min. First live use of
this pattern anywhere in the course (Course-Curriculum.md §1, §7 item
#18) — spend the first few minutes explaining it before running it.
Recurs on Weeks 7, 8, 9, and 10 without needing to be re-taught once
this class has done it once.*

---

## Why this exists

- Borrowed from agile stand-ups, but built for **matching**, not just
  status reporting.
- The point isn't "did everyone report in" — it's getting a stuck team
  paired with a team that can actually help.

---

## Write *(~5 min)*

- Each team posts a **3-line note** to their notes-KB:
  1. What we finished since last time.
  2. What we're stuck on.
  3. What we need.

---

## Match *(~5–10 min)*

- Instructor scans the room/notes quickly.
- Calls out **2–3 pairings** — a team's blocker matched to another
  team's recent win or visible expertise.
- Those teams huddle briefly to troubleshoot together.

> Speaker notes: common Week 5 pairings to expect — a team confused
> about 3.3V vs. 5V paired with one that already sorted it out; a team
> getting garbage firmware readings paired with one whose sensor read
> is already clean.

---

## Optional public share

- Time permitting, one sentence to the whole room: *"anyone stuck on
  X, Team Y just solved it."*
- Skip this if the room is short on time — Match is the part that
  actually matters.
